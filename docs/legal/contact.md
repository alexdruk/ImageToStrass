# Contact

How to reach PatternNifty.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

## Questions about an order or a design

Send us a message on Etsy: open [the PatternNifty listing](IS_ETSY_LISTING_URL) (plain words when IS_ETSY_LISTING_URL is not set) and use Message seller. Please include your PatternNifty code (PN-XXXX-XXXX).

## Privacy requests

To see, correct or delete your data, email `IS_CONTACT_EMAIL` with your code. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

PatternNifty has no contact form: order questions go through Etsy, so they stay with your order.

---

`IS_…` names are settings in `.env`: the site shows their values, or "Not set" when one is empty.
